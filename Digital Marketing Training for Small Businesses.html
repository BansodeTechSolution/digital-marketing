Good to know

Do I need a website?

Not to begin. You can start with accurate social profiles and local listings, then consider a simple website once it has a clear purpose.

Do I need a big budget?

No. We start with free and low-cost methods and only plan paid ads with a clear goal and spending limit.

How much time will it take?

The weekly routine is designed for busy owners: a few short, planned tasks rather than daily work.

Will my customer information be safe?

Yes. Sessions use sample material, and we teach permission-based messaging that respects customer privacy.

Why we built it

Small businesses power local communities, but many find digital marketing confusing or hard to keep up. This project turns survey feedback from local business owners into practical, beginner-level training that starts with familiar tools and focuses on useful habits.
